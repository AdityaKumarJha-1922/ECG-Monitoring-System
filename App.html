Heart Rate Reference:

✓ Normal: 60-100 BPM (Adults at rest)

⚠ Low (Bradycardia): 40-60 BPM

⚠ High (Tachycardia): 100-150 BPM

✗ Very Low: Below 40 BPM

✗ Very High: Above 150 BPM

How to use:

  1. Connect your ESP32 device with AD8232 ECG sensor
  2. View real-time heart rate data on Ubidots dashboard
  3. The device automatically calculates heart rate and determines if it's normal
  4. Heart rate status is updated every 0.5 seconds

Note: To view real-time data from your ESP32 device, please access your Ubidots dashboard at industrial.ubidots.com
